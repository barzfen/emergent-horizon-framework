# Contributing and acknowledging others

EHF welcomes corrections, independent reproduction, counterexamples, extensions and relevant literature. A contribution need not support an EHF hypothesis to be useful. Follow the evidence labels in the main README and the licensing scope in [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md).

## Credit at the point of use

- Cite the original researchers when a definition, theorem, method, dataset, code fragment or figure informs the work. Give authors, title, year, a primary-source link or DOI, and the version inspected where relevant.
- State whether we read the full work, only its abstract, or a secondary account. Do not imply that we verified a proof when we only tested examples.
- Separate an author's result from our reproduction, adaptation or new observation. Identify changes and avoid presenting rediscovery as novelty. Check the relevant literature before making a priority claim.
- Put citations beside the claims they support and retain a bibliography for longer notes. Add source comments beside adapted code and record dataset provenance.
- Preserve existing copyright, licence and permission notices. Check reuse permissions before copying code or figures; attribution alone does not supply permission. Linking and summarizing may be more appropriate than redistributing a paper.
- Name actual contributors accurately. Acknowledging someone's research does not imply they endorse EHF, collaborated with us or reviewed our results.

## Reproducibility and contribution records

State assumptions, model definitions, parameters, inputs, commands, results and limitations. Include negative outcomes. Document AI assistance and its scope when material; human maintainers remain responsible for checking sources and claims. Identify any third-party material and its terms rather than applying EHF's defaults to it. Submit only material you are entitled to contribute under its stated terms.

## Potential contribution back

Each [related-work entry](research/related-work/README.md) should record whether there is something useful to return to the source researchers: a reproduction, a counterexample, an extension, reusable code or a precise question. It is acceptable to conclude that there is nothing ready to share.

Before proposing outreach, check the result, its provenance and the relevant prior literature. Prepare a concise explanation with reproducible evidence, give the original work clear credit and distinguish established findings from questions. Contact requires an explicit instruction from the maintainer; a log entry is not authorization to send messages.

## Citing EHF

Use [CITATION.cff](CITATION.cff) for repository-level metadata, or cite the specific note or probe, its actual authors and a stable revision. A contributor's scientific credit should follow their actual contribution; the repository-level citation does not replace attribution to other researchers.
