# EHF Holonomy Branch

This branch investigates whether nontrivial loop holonomy can arise from EHF's existing primitive structure of admissible comparison and transformation.

## Research discipline

The programme keeps three levels separate:

1. **Mathematical result** — properties proved or measured in an EHF construction.
2. **Geometric analogy** — comparison with established notions such as parallel transport, holonomy, and curvature.
3. **Physical interpretation** — any possible relation to gravity or emergent spacetime. This remains speculative unless independently derived and empirically constrained.

The branch does **not** assume that EHF describes gravity, that spacetime is fundamental, or that an analogy with general relativity establishes physical equivalence.

## Motivation

General relativity is locally inertial but generally not globally reducible to one inertial frame. One operational signature of curvature is path-dependent parallel transport: transport around a closed loop need not return an object to its original orientation.

EHF already treats admissible comparison as primitive and studies histories and cycles. The natural substrate-independent question is therefore whether composition of existing comparison maps around closed admissible histories is trivial or nontrivial.

For a closed history

\[
\gamma: S_1\to S_2\to\cdots\to S_n\to S_1,
\]

define a transport/comparison composition

\[
H(\gamma)=C_{n1}C_{n-1,n}\cdots C_{12}.
\]

The first question is simply whether

\[
H(\gamma)=I
\]

for all admissible closed histories, or whether some sectors admit

\[
H(\gamma)\ne I.
\]

No gravitational interpretation is required for either outcome.
