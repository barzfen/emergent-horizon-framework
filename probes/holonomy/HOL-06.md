# EHF-HOL-06 — Representation-Sector Generator Audit

**Status: PROPOSED / ANALYTIC TARGET IDENTIFIED — the existing RECUR association-scheme decomposition supplies the correct coordinates, but a sectorwise generator theorem has not yet been proved.**

## Motivation

HOL-05 found two asymptotic scales for native growth:

- full loop-intertwining defect `D_n = H_N U - U H_n` is numerically consistent with `O(1/q)` in Frobenius norm;
- the distinguished nonreal unitary rotation changes only as `Delta theta_q = O(1/q^2)`, with `q=n-2` and `q^2 Delta theta_q -> 3`.

A single scalar normalization therefore cannot describe every part of the growing observer operator. The natural next step is to use the representation sectors already present in RECUR-08 rather than compare growing matrices entry by entry.

## Existing exact decomposition

For Hamilton-cycle one-port observers, fix two anchors `a,b` and let `R` be the `q=n-2` remaining labels. RECUR-08 splits the pair-state transport into two invariant combinatorial blocks.

### X block

States containing the opposite anchor are indexed by one label in `R`. The raw block is `J_q-I_q`, whose polar factor is

`W_X = 2 J_q/q - I_q`.

This decomposes into:

- the trivial one-dimensional span of the all-ones vector, eigenvalue `+1`;
- the `(q-1)`-dimensional standard subspace orthogonal to it, eigenvalue `-1`.

### Y block

States using two labels of `R` live in the Johnson scheme `J(q,2)`. If `N` is the incidence matrix from 2-subsets to labels and

`Z = N - (2/q) 1`,

then the polar factor is

`W_Y = P_0 - P_1`,

where `P_0` projects onto the trivial sector and `P_1` onto the standard sector generated by centered label incidence. The remaining Johnson sector is kernel.

Thus the HC transport already comes with a canonical symmetry decomposition into trivial, standard and residual/kernel sectors.

For perfect-matching observers the transport consists of one deterministic anchor-swap channel plus the same reflection-type block

`2 J_q/q - I_q`

on the remaining labels. This provides an independent control with a much smaller state space.

## Loop spectrum already implied by these sectors

RECUR-08 established that the lossless three-observer loop has spectrum

`{1, (-1)^[...], exp(+i theta_n), exp(-i theta_n)}`

with

`cos(theta_n) = [3q^2-4]/q^3`.

The nonreal pair is therefore a low-dimensional symmetry-sector effect, whereas large standard/residual multiplicities account for much of the total observer dimension.

This makes the HOL-05 scale separation plausible: an `O(q^-1)` rearrangement can live in growing standard/complement sectors while the special two-dimensional rotation pair drifts only at `O(q^-2)`.

## HOL-06 questions

Construct orthonormal symmetry-adapted bases at levels `n` and `N` and express the native growth embedding `U`, old loop `H_n`, new loop `H_N`, and defect

`D_n = H_N U - U H_n`

in those bases.

For each source/target sector pair `(r,s)`, compute

`D_n^(r->s) = P_s^(N) D_n P_r^(n)`.

Audit:

1. which blocks are identically zero by symmetry;
2. which blocks have rank bounded independently of `n`;
3. which blocks scale as `1/q`;
4. whether the distinguished rotation block scales as `1/q^2`;
5. whether `q D_n^(r->s)` or `q^2 D_n^(r->s)` has an exact finite-dimensional limit;
6. whether HC and PM share the same reduced generator after multiplicity spaces are factored out.

## Required standard of proof

A numerical projection is useful only as reconnaissance. The target is an analytic statement derived from the exact `J`, incidence, Johnson-projector and growth formulas.

The preferred result would have the form

`D_n = q^-1 A_n + q^-2 B_n + O(q^-3)`

where symmetry identifies finite reduced matrices `A` and `B`, with multiplicity spaces separated explicitly. In particular, the distinguished rotation sector should reproduce

`q^2 Delta theta_q -> 3`

without conflating that second-order spectral drift with the first-order full-space obstruction.

## Gauge / basis discipline

Individual matrix entries inside a degenerate standard sector depend on basis choice. Claims must therefore use invariant sector data: singular values, reduced intertwiner coefficients, traces on irreducible blocks, ranks, or conjugacy classes. No preferred coordinate basis is to be treated as physical.

## Interpretive boundary

Even a successful sectorwise generator theorem would establish a generator of **observer-comparison change under combinatorial growth**. It would not by itself establish a Hamiltonian time generator, spacetime connection, curvature tensor, or physical field equation.

## Completion criterion

HOL-06 passes only if at least one nonzero sector admits a controlled analytic rescaling limit and the result explains the coexistence of the `1/q` full defect with the `1/q^2` rotation drift. Otherwise the generator interpretation remains a finite-size numerical analogy.
