## Finding

Reopening the bridge is justified as a research programme. The later constructions provide a more explicit substrate, access restrictions, predictive equivalence, local readout and a represented interaction. These sharpen the question of whether quantum mechanics could be an effective description. They have not yet supplied a rule selecting coherent dynamics over stochastic dynamics.

This is a bounded source audit and a new six-state control calculation. It is not a review of every later EHF branch. AMP, RAW/GROW, Wheeler and the newer holonomy series have not been re-audited here. No physical identification, novelty claim or external researcher assessment is made.

## New calculation: one carrier, two supplied dynamics

Independently reconstruct the fixed seven-vertex carrier with type-1 vertices {1,2,3}. Canonical cycles are anchored at 0 and quotient reflection. Moves are circular LR2/LR3 reversals preserving the canonical Pi partition and cyclic no-isolation.

The retained MICRO–OPW23 component has six states and seven distinct undirected edges. LR3 alone divides it into components A (four states) and B (two states). The number of type-1 neighbours at vertex 0 is one throughout A and zero throughout B. Thus B membership is a specific binary local readout.

Let L be this graph's combinatorial Laplacian. For this new benchmark choose rate one per distinct edge and compare:

- Classical probability evolution: p(t)=exp(-t L)p(0).
- Coherent control: psi(t)=exp(-i t L)psi(0), read out with squared magnitudes.

The second rule is deliberately ADDED. It is not inferred by replacing a symbol in the first rule. The units and distinct-edge rate convention are supplied; they differ from accepted-start-event sampling and from MICRO–OPW 03's 42-state joint process.

Choose the previously retained adjacent cycles a=(0,1,2,3,4,5,6) and b=(0,5,4,3,2,1,6), belonging to A and B respectively. Prepare the two coherent controls

    psi_plus  = (|a> + i|b>)/sqrt(2)
    psi_minus = (|a> - i|b>)/sqrt(2).

They have IDENTICAL initial probabilities over every carrier state: one half at a and one half at b. Under the coherent control their B-readout probabilities differ:

| Supplied dynamics/preparation | B probability at benchmark t=0.5 |
|---|---:|
| Classical Markov mixture | 0.466371818249 |
| Coherent plus phase | 0.879313248492 |
| Coherent minus phase | 0.106516078487 |
| Incoherent mixture evolved by the unitary control | 0.492914663489 |

The coherent contrast at this sampled time is 0.772797170005. It is not an optimized maximum or a measured physical effect. Mixing the plus and minus preparations equally gives the incoherent unitary result at every checked time; this checks the phase/interference comparison separately from the Markov comparison.

An elementary exact check explains why the contrast is real within the control: the initial B-probability derivatives for the two phases are +1 and -1 in the chosen units, since a and b are connected with H_ab=-1. This calculation uses the supplied complex phase and Schrödinger rule. It is a benchmark for what a deeper mechanism must reproduce, not a discovery of phase in the original carrier.

## Two precise boundaries

### A stochastic state description has no independent phase coordinate

For a fixed Markov kernel and fixed endpoint readout r, the outcome probability is r^T exp(tQ)p. Identical full initial p therefore gives identical endpoint statistics. Positive path contributions add; there is no relative phase cancellation among them.

This does NOT rule out a richer EHF substrate. Two preparations with the same coarse observation can have different distributions over hidden states, different apparatus states or histories, and then different future laws. The observer audits already give examples of this classical phenomenon. Such a difference alone is not an interference witness. A renewed bridge must state exactly what full state is retained, which preparations are operationally equivalent, and how the proposed phase is encoded.

### Static coarse readouts alone do not supply incompatible measurements

Every deterministic subset readout on these six carrier states is represented by a diagonal projector. All 64 such projectors commute; the script checks all 4,096 ordered pairs exactly with integer matrices. The general reason is simply that diagonal matrices commute.

This statement applies to passive readouts on the same fixed carrier basis. Sequential interactions can be order-dependent in classical models; disturbance alone does not establish quantum incompatibility. Non-diagonal Heisenberg observables can arise after the supplied unitary control, but their existence then depends on that additional dynamics. An operational measurement mechanism must be represented and tested rather than inferred from coarse-graining alone.

## Proposed next probe: substrate-derived phase and recombination

Keep the six-state model as a small negative/control benchmark. It may prove too small for the needed mechanism; enlargement should retain an explicit map back to its carrier and readout.

1. Specify an additional retained EHF history or comparison variable, if needed, using actual admissible moves. State whether it is derived, chosen or imported. Do not assign complex phase merely to obtain cancellation.
2. Construct actual preparation, route selection and recombination interactions. Keep distinguishable histories separate; erase records only through represented dynamics.
3. Compare with an explicit classical hidden-state/process model on the same complete state and the same observation protocol. If it explains all outcomes, record that result.
4. Test whether the proposed structure predicts phase-like composition and cancellation, and whether a quadratic readout follows from independently stated requirements. Distinguish these requirements from the old assumed Hilbert/unitary geometry.
5. If a coherent effective description succeeds, identify its error and breakdown regime under leakage, coarse-graining and imperfect records. This is where the hypothesis that QM is effective becomes testable within the construction.

Composition, incompatible measurement contexts and unfiltered Bell tests remain subsequent targets. They require a separately specified composite state space, local operations and preparation assumptions; none is inferred from this single-carrier calculation.

A useful failure is that every operational effect is captured by a classical hidden Markov model with explicit disturbance. A useful intermediate success is a derived, controlled effective amplitude description. A stronger success would explain why that description and its probabilities are selected, rather than merely showing that they can be attached to a graph.
