# Same-colour label parity on the MICRO reversal graph

**Status.** Exact finite graph calculations, a sign-transport argument and a numerical coherent-walk comparison. The original nine-site exploratory code supplied complex phases from binary inversion parity. Here the sign comes from tracked tile labels. The coherent walk is a supplied dynamical model.

## Carrier and sign

Use the 18 rooted binary rings of length nine with four ones that satisfy MICRO. There are no isolated bits. Keep the fixed slot coordinate frame and use cyclic reversals of lengths 2 through 7. Count each start and length as a separate event. Keep events that change the visible word and preserve MICRO. There are 126 undirected events on 81 distinct links.

Within each visible word, number the labels of each colour in ascending slot order. A reversal transports those labels. In the target word, read each colour in ascending slot order and take the permutation parity relative to its reference order. This gives a pair of signs, one per colour. Choosing a different label reference at a vertex changes the edge signs there by a common vertex factor. Products along closed histories are independent of the reference, since the factors cancel at intermediate vertices. Composition of the actual permutations gives the same history sign.

Equivalently, start with the unsigned event operator on fully labelled rings. Restrict to vectors that transform under swaps of zero labels by their permutation sign, under swaps of one labels by their permutation sign, or under both. The induced matrix on binary words sums the corresponding sign over all events taking one visible word to another. This explains the signed matrix once a symmetry sector of the coherent model is chosen.

## Exact witnesses

Slots are zero-indexed. From `000001111`, reverse five slots starting at 4 to get `000011110`. Then reverse seven slots starting at 3. The visible word is back at the start. The labels at slots 0 and 3 have swapped, and all other labels have returned. Thus this closed history has odd zero-label parity and even one-label parity.

Two different single events take `000001111` to `000111100`. The six-slot reversal at 3 has signs (−,+), and the seven-slot reversal at 7 has signs (+,−). In either one-colour signed model their amplitudes sum to zero. This is exact cancellation of a matrix entry. Longer routes can still populate the target at later times.

After summing events, the zero-label signed matrix has a square with edge weights +1, +1, +1, −1:

`000001111 → 000011110 → 000111100 → 001111000 → 000001111`.

The negative product survives vertex rephasing and an overall sign flip of the Hamiltonian, since the square has even length. Separately, signing all labels leaves every link present but reduces 18 link magnitudes compared with the unsigned event-count matrix.

## Coherent comparison

Use each signed real symmetric matrix H as a Hamiltonian with unit magnitude per reversal event before summing. Start from `000001111`. Compare ring-outcome probabilities with the walk generated by the entrywise absolute value |H|, so all link magnitudes, including zeros, are shared. Total variation distance ranges from zero to one.

| Signs used | Links cancelled | Distance at t = 0.5 | Distance at t = 1 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Zero labels | 9 of 81 | 0.739500 | 0.156156 |
| One labels | 9 of 81 | 0.432959 | 0.314623 |
| Both colours | 0 of 81 | 0 | 0 |

The both-colour equality holds exactly at all times for a basis-state start and ring-basis probabilities. The verifier finds a diagonal sign matrix D with H = −D|H|D. Vertex signs do not affect these probabilities, and the global minus sign conjugates evolution generated by a real matrix. This control shows why a negative closed loop alone is insufficient to infer a change in this readout.

Restricting to reversals of lengths 2–4 gives phase-free ring-basis probabilities for all three sign choices in this carrier. The verifier finds H = D|H|D for zero labels and H = −D|H|D for the other two choices. The detectable sign effect in the table therefore depends on the move set.

The labelled permutations and their parities exist in a classical history. A coherent preparation in a chosen symmetry sector is the extra ingredient that lets signs combine as amplitudes. The calculation establishes neither a physical mechanism selecting that sector nor the continuous phase supplied in the original exploratory code.

## Reproduce

Run `python3 probes/qm-bridge/label-parity/probe.py` with NumPy and SciPy. It verifies the permutations and cancellations, checks the sign conjugacies with integer matrices and reproduces the numerical distances.

This probe uses rooted binary words and all visible-changing MICRO reversals in its length range. The earlier [orientation transport study](../revisit-02/README.md) used a directional reference on MICRO–OPW tours. The [reversal admissibility audit](../../reversals/all-length-01/README.md) uses distinct labelled cycles modulo rotation and reflection.
