# WHEELER-30 — Clock-Naturality / Local-Rate Uniqueness Audit

> **Review correction — 18 October 2026:** The linear rate derived from insertion sites is the **growth rate R_G**, not automatically the **comparison rate R_C**. Every substitution into the Core-C comparison gap below additionally assumes a scheduler with R_C=R_G. That connection has not been derived. The finite limit is a conditional frozen-layer relaxation result, not the gap of the full growing process. See [ERRATA-01](ERRATA-01.md).

**Status: PARTIAL PASS; linear total rate is forced by symmetry/locality assumptions up to one overall clock constant**

## Question

Given the all-slot Hamilton-cycle growth rule, do relabelling symmetry, locality, subsystem additivity, growth consistency, and absence of global-size information at a local site force a unique scaling of total growth/comparison rate?

## Setup

A Hamilton cycle of size `n` has exactly `n` admissible insertion sites, one per cycle edge.

Let the local event rate at each site be `r_n`. Relabelling symmetry makes all sites equivalent at fixed `n`, so the total rate is

`R_n = n r_n`.

## Growth consistency

Under growth `n -> n+1`, the old `n` sites persist as local structures inside the enlarged cycle (with one local neighbourhood modified where the new label is inserted). If an untouched local site has no access to the global size of the state, then its elementary clock rate cannot change merely because a distant insertion occurred.

Hence for untouched sites,

`r_{n+1} = r_n`.

Iterating gives

`r_n = r`,

where `r` is a size-independent constant that fixes only the overall unit of time.

Therefore

`R_n = n r`.

## Consequence for the Core-C spectral law

From WHEELER-27/28,

`d_n = n(n-3)/2`,

and the combinatorial Laplacian gap is

`lambda_n = n-1`.

With total event rate `R_n = nr`, the continuous-time effective gap is

`Delta_n = R_n * lambda_n / d_n`

`= nr * 2(n-1)/(n(n-3))`

`= 2r (n-1)/(n-3)`.

Thus

`Delta_n -> 2r`.

The continuum limiting gap is finite and nonzero.

## What is actually proved

Under the assumptions:

1. relabelling symmetry of elementary sites;
2. locality of the elementary clock;
3. no explicit access by a local event to the global size `n`;
4. additive total hazard/rate over independent elementary sites;
5. growth consistency for unchanged local sites;

one obtains uniquely

`R_n = nr`

up to one overall positive multiplicative constant `r`.

This is not an arbitrary normalization chosen to preserve the gap: the linear scaling follows from the number of equivalent local growth sites plus consistency of their local rate.

## Limitation

The conclusion is conditional on an additive/Markovian local-rate picture. EHF has not yet proved that internal time must be generated by independent Poisson-like local clocks. Cooperative multi-site events, nonlocal scheduling, or history-dependent rates could evade the argument.

Therefore the result is best classified as a conditional derivation of the critical rate law, not yet a fundamental theorem about EHF time.

## Verdict

- **PASS:** relabelling symmetry implies a common local rate at fixed size.
- **PASS:** locality + no global-size access + growth consistency force that local rate to be size-independent.
- **PASS:** additive local hazards then force `R_n = nr`.
- **PASS:** this yields `Delta_n -> 2r` for Core C.
- **UNRESOLVED:** whether EHF internal time is necessarily additive/Markovian in this sense.
