Recovered source package, 2 November 2026. [Sources and probe specifications](sources/) cover QM3-01 through QM3-05: intrinsic projectors, degenerate slow multiplets, chart-dependent access, finite symmetry representations and product-permutahedron scaling.

These files support the [OPW3 working document](../sources/ehf_opw3_rebuild_working_document_v1_6.md). A complex vector-space lift and a chosen operator do not derive physical quantum mechanics. Historical source verdicts have not been rerun in this consolidation. Generated original certificates may still be missing; see the [import scope](../../consolidation-2026-11-02/README.md).
