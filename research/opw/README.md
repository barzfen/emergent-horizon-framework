# Odd Preserve World: OPW3 and OPW23

Current navigation note: 2 November 2026. These are two explicit mathematical models, not competing established theories of nature.

Both use undirected labelled Hamilton cycles with a declared canonical chart and a preserved even/odd-position label partition Pi. OPW3 permits circular length-3 reversals preserving Pi. OPW23 permits length-2 and length-3 reversals preserving Pi. The state object, admissibility filter, transition probabilities/rates and observer must be specified separately.

## Current comparative position

The retained baseline checks at n=5,7,9 find identical OPW3/OPW23 chamber partitions. At n=9 the edge counts are 53,760 versus 67,200 over 20,160 states and 69 chambers. Extra LR2 edges change internal dynamics and slow eigenspaces; equality of component partitions is not equality of relaxation or observation.

Treat OPW3 as the simpler reference and OPW23 as a distinct research model. No evidence selects either as nature's move set. The old sixteen-signature packet matrix is an aggregated description: exact edge counts do not establish exact multistep Markov closure. The OPW3 rebuild explicitly audits equitable refinements and hidden slow modes.

**Convention warning:** RAW/GROW canonical reversal-support channels identify complementary supports; their symbol LR3 is not interchangeable at all sizes with a literal circular length-3 reversal in OPW. For example, GROW's canonical support-3 onset at size 6 does not contradict OPW's literal length-3 moves at size 5. Declare the convention before transferring a claim.
