# EHF-OPW3-AUDIT-03  
## Normalized-Laplacian Slow-Sector Survival

### Purpose

Determine whether the slow structural modes previously studied on OPW23 are
already present in the foundational LR3-only graph, and quantify how much the
LR2 enrichment changes them.

This audit uses the symmetric normalized Laplacian

\[
L_{\mathrm{norm}}
=
I-D^{-1/2}AD^{-1/2}.
\]

Its eigenvalues are structural diffusion rates. They are not yet interpreted as
physical energies.

### Exact comparison basis

Audit-01 proved at \(n=5,7,9\) that OPW3 and OPW23 have the same chamber
partition. Therefore each OPW3 chamber can be compared directly with the
OPW23 chamber on the identical vertex set.

For every chamber the probe computes:

- the retained low normalized-Laplacian eigenvalues;
- the spectral gap;
- inverse participation ratios;
- the first \(r\)-dimensional nontrivial slow subspace;
- principal-angle alignment between the OPW3 and OPW23 slow subspaces.

### Signature-observable audit

For each graph and chamber, the probe constructs stationary-measure-centered
observable subspaces generated by:

1. the four LR3 active signatures;
2. the four LR2 active signatures;
3. the joint sixteen-signature crosswalk.

For each retained slow mode it records the squared projection onto each
observable subspace.

This tests whether the old active-signature variables actually see the slow
structural sector. It does not assume that they form an exact Markov quotient.

### Interpretation discipline

A pass establishes numerical and structural consistency of the spectral
calculation. The scientifically relevant quantities are:

- OPW3 versus OPW23 gap changes;
- slow-subspace principal-angle alignment;
- localization of low modes;
- visibility or blindness of slow modes to the 4-state and 16-state signature
  observables.

No particle or quantum claim follows from a slow normalized-Laplacian mode by
itself.
