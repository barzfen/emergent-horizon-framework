## Setup
Balanced fixed colour counts A,B,C. Nine vertices have 3 of each; twelve vertices have 4 of each. A colour word is admissible if every cyclic adjacent pair differs. LR3 swaps positions i and i+2, subject to the target remaining admissible. Colour bracelets quotient rotations and reversal; full labelled Hamiltonian cycles retain distinct vertex labels within each colour.

The unique frozen bracelet at n=9 is ABCABCABC and at n=12 ABCABCABCABC. A singleton quotient component is not necessarily frozen: legal LR3 transitions can remain within that bracelet.

## Full labelled n=9 audit
Fix labelled colour classes A={0,1,2}, B={3,4,5}, C={6,7,8}. Enumerate each admissible colour word and each bijective assignment of colour labels, quotient cycle rotation/reversal, and retain edges generated by legal LR3. There are 1584 admissible labelled undirected cycles. The exact LR3 component distribution is:
- 72 isolated singletons (72 labelled cycles)
- 162 components of size 4 (648 labelled cycles)
- 1 component of size 864
Total 235 components and 1584 states. Legal degree distribution: 72 states degree 0; 648 degree 2; 648 degree 5; 216 degree 9.

This shows the 5-component bracelet quotient conceals fragmentation into 235 labelled components. A quotient's connectivity is not equivalent to connectivity of its underlying labelled graph.

## Reproducible algorithm
Generate all balanced words using combinations of A positions and B positions, fill remaining C. Filter cyclic separation. Canonicalize bracelet by minimum rotation/reversal. For each bracelet representative, generate nine (or twelve) LR3 endpoint swaps and retain those passing separation; count connected components of the quotient.

For full labelled n=9, enumerate 3! permutations of labels for each of the three colours for each valid word, canonicalize the undirected labelled cycle by rotation to label 0 and lexicographically smaller traversal, deduplicate, generate legal LR3 neighbours and run DFS/BFS on the resulting 1584-state graph.

## Caveats
The n=12 full labelled graph has not been enumerated here; the 23-component result applies only to colour bracelets. Three classical colours are not SU(3) or QCD colour charges. The separation rule and fixed balanced counts are modelling choices, not derived physical laws.
