# EHF-CBB-TRI-07 — Exact sign-word rewriting and symmetry quotient

Date: 2026-11-10. Status: local theorem and executed n=15 exhaustive check.

## Sign encoding
Fix the oriented cyclic colour order A->B->C->A. For any proper cyclic three-colour word w, define s_i=+ if the edge w_i->w_(i+1) advances one colour modulo 3, and s_i=- otherwise. The signed sum is divisible by 3 because the word closes; W=(sum s_i)/3. Conversely, a sign word whose sum is divisible by 3 reconstructs a proper cyclic colour word after choosing one initial colour (three possibilities). Balance of colour multiplicities is an additional restriction, not implied by sign closure.

Rotation rotates the sign word. Reversal reverses the sign order AND interchanges + and -. Thus the sign-word bracelet equivalence is generated by rotation and reverse-complement, not plain reversal.

## Exact local rewrite theorem
A colour-changing separation-admissible LR3 move has the five-letter form XYXZX <-> XZXYX, with X,Y,Z all distinct. Its four edge signs transform as
+--+ <-> -++-
(or the reverse direction). This is the *only* nontrivial sign-word rewrite. All other sign positions remain unchanged. It preserves the total signed sum and hence winding. It also preserves colour multiplicities, because it merely swaps two colours at positions.

## n=15 balanced enumeration
Enumerating 5196 separation-admissible linear colour words with five each A,B,C gives 174 dihedral colour bracelets and 56 connected components under legal LR3, as in TRI-05.

Map these 174 colour bracelets to canonical sign words under rotation and reverse-complement. The result is 60 sign bracelets: 57 sign classes each have three colour-bracelet preimages, and three have one preimage. The induced sign rewrite graph has 20 connected components, with component-size multiset:
- ten singleton components
- four components of size five
- three components of size three
- one each of sizes two, eight and eleven.
Sum 10+20+9+2+8+11=60.

The direct +--+ <-> -++- rewrite graph was checked to match the image of colour-changing LR3 transitions, after omitting self-class edges from same-colour swaps. Sign-graph connectivity is not equivalent to colour-bracelet connectivity: quotienting away the global colour origin merges states and can merge disconnected sectors. The 20 sign components should not be reported as the original 56 sectors.

## Reproducible algorithm
1. Generate proper balanced words via all A-position combinations, then B-position combinations, fill C.
2. Canonicalize colour bracelets under rotation/reversal; generate legal LR3 swaps and connected components.
3. Encode each colour bracelet as a +/- sign string using cyclic A->B->C->A increments.
4. Canonicalize sign strings under rotations and reverse-complement.
5. Build the sign graph using only four-sign rewrites +--+ <-> -++-; compare edges with projected colour LR3 graph, ignoring projected self-edges from same-colour label swaps.
6. Assert 174 colour bracelets, 60 sign bracelets, 56 colour components, 20 sign components.

## Interpretation and next
The sign-word representation is an exact encoding up to a choice of initial colour, and gives a minimal local rewrite alphabet of two symbols. It is not yet a complete classification of the connected components: the global colour-origin degree of freedom and potentially further invariants matter. Next investigate combinatorial invariants of the +--+ <-> -++- rewrite (e.g. conserved cyclic defect order), and determine when its sign-component lifts split into multiple colour components. No QCD/SU(3) claim.
