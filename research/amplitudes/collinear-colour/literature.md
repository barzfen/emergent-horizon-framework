# Literature search for the planar colour-ratio identity

Search date 6 November 2026. The target was the exact full-colour SU(3) ratio of projected squared norms for the first finite equal-split MHV collinear coefficient, on the real-projective-spinor locus. Searches combined “subleading collinear”, “finite”, “MHV”, “colour multiplet”, “singlet”, “symmetric octet”, “27”, “Casimir”, “coplanar” and “1:2:3”. Related work was checked on colour decomposition, colour-resolved norms, and finite collinear amplitude relations.

| Work | Relevant overlap |
| --- | --- |
| [Del Duca, Dixon and Maltoni, *New Color Decompositions for Gauge Amplitudes at Tree and Loop Level*](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/9910563) | Provides the structure-constant basis used for the full amplitude and the spectator commutator reduction. |
| [Dokshitzer and Marchesini, *Soft gluons at large angles in hadron collisions*](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0509078) and [Keppeler and Sjödahl, *Orthogonal multiplet bases in SU(Nc) color space*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1207.0609) | Supply the representation and Casimir machinery. The four-point starting ratio follows from the usual dimensions and colour-operator eigenvalues. |
| [Stieberger and Taylor, *Subleading Terms in the Collinear Limit of Yang–Mills Amplitudes*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1508.01116) | Relates finite colour-ordered collinear terms to one-graviton Einstein–Yang–Mills amplitudes. A close precedent for the finite term itself. |
| [Nandan, Plefka and Wormsbecher, *Collinear limits beyond the leading order from the scattering equations*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1608.04730) | Derives a subleading gluon collinear kernel in the scattering-equation formulation. |
| [Dong and Stieberger, *Subleading Collinear Limits of Yang–Mills Amplitudes from Recursions*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00120) and [*Subleading Collinear Limits of Yang-Mills Amplitudes from Gravity*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00153) | The first describes finite adjacent-collinear coefficients using recursion. The second describes relations and a reduced basis for strict equal-helicity collinear data. It is the closest lead for checking whether the planar quadratic identities follow from known constraints on the data. |
| [Frederix, Hirschi and Sjödahl, *Exact color sums for multi-gluon amplitudes*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39574) | Recent precedent for computing exact tree-level colour sums using adjoint and multiplet methods. Its abstract describes a general evaluation benchmark. |
| [Harland-Lang, *Planar radiation zeros in five-parton QCD amplitudes*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.06798) | Uses coplanar real kinematics and MHV relations to derive amplitude zeros. This is a precedent for kinematic planarity constraining colour amplitudes, with a different quantity and claim. |

The sources located establish the ingredients and related identities. This search found no explicit statement of the all-multiplicity 1:2:3 projected norm law or of the spectator-pole induction for it. Coverage of some recent preprints was limited to abstracts or indexed text, and the result may follow from known relations in a different basis. Novelty remains unverified. The result to compare with prior work is the pair of quadratic norm identities and their real-kinematics condition, rather than the existence of finite collinear terms or the use of multiplet projectors.
