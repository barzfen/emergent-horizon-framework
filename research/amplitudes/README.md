# Amplitude studies

| Series | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| [Nine-point ordering and residue studies](nine-point-tail/README.md) | Parity fibres, ordering cuts, exact liftability certificates and a gluon-amplitude benchmark. |
| [Collinear colour channels](collinear-colour/README.md) | The SU(3) planar ratio, its SU(N) extension, the real Lorentzian counterexample and the six-gluon near-planar sign proof. |

The ordering studies ask which identities lift through prescribed cyclic supports. The colour studies ask what the leading and first finite collinear coefficients retain about the gluon pair. The two series share a question about information lost under a reduction. A direct correspondence between the ordering cuts and the colour channels remains open.

[Orientation and ordering bridge](ordering-orientation-bridge-01.md) compares event-labelled Hamiltonian orientation, nine-point tail support-cut ranks, and collinear exchange parity. It proposes an exact signed-residue equivariance test; it does **not** derive the 1:2:3 norm law from holonomy.

[Signed orientation and tail support audit](nine-point-tail/followups/signed-orientation-bridge.md) gives an exact 40,320-word reflection/residue commutation check and shows why reflection closure does not repair the six tail-rank classes. The rank-14 two-word support repair is independently reproduced.

[Rank-14 repair-pair audit](nine-point-tail/followups/rank14-event-pairs.md) exhaustively classifies two-word support repairs in all six rank-14 tail fibres; a fitted label-swap template filters candidates but cannot replace the KK quotient cancellation criterion. Includes exact code, held-out fibre checks and preserved pair data.

[Minimal rank-14 repair-distance audit](nine-point-tail/followups/rank14-minimal-triples.md) completes the exact nine-point two-versus-three extra-word classification, with explicit KK relations, a lossless finite-field candidate screen followed by rational checks, and a reconstruction-support-distance formulation.

[Canonical LR3 support threshold](nine-point-tail/followups/rank14-lr3-threshold.md) proves a finite six-fibre rule distinguishing two-word versus three-word rank-14 tail repairs: a fixed LR3 reversal centred on deleted label 9 works iff the ordering comparison counts satisfy κ₁=2 or κ₃=4. The proof uses two exact KK seeds and support monotonicity; it is not an all-multiplicity or Casimir-ratio theorem.
