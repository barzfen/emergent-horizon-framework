# EHF recovered source documents

Original working documents supplied by Barry Fensom on 4 November 2026. Their contents are preserved byte-for-byte under readable filenames. [manifest.json](manifest.json) records the original filenames, sizes, SHA-256 digests and Git blob identifiers. The nine-point theorem was already present and is linked rather than duplicated.

These are cumulative research records, not newly validated papers. Embedded labels such as PASS, exact, proved or authoritative retain their historical meaning; this import does not independently endorse every claim. Referenced scripts, certificates and datasets are not automatically included with these documents. Filenames identify supplied snapshots; opening headers may describe earlier stages.

## Lam and amplitude reading guide

Search the section identifiers within [PHYS-ALG v0.65](phys-alg-v0.65.md):

- **BERG-03–05:** oriented-matroid/Bergman geometry and alternating corank-one cases. Local fan contributions must be distinguished from whole intersection forms.
- **XKLT-03:** corrects earlier complete-graph indexing: N-point amplitudes use M(K_(N-1)). Reports an explicit cycle-ordering/tope dictionary and the nine-point bounded-tope source identification. This does not identify LR3 adjacency with tope adjacency.
- **XKLT-04:** places the six nine-point deletion images inside one fixed 15-dimensional local amplitude-relation space. Reports generic rank 57 using exact upper bounds and finite-field minor witnesses. The six spaces are not six non-null KLT amplitude blocks.
- **XKLT-05:** reports a boundary-capacity mechanism for the nine-point image dimensions.
- **XKLT-07A–C:** eleven-to-ten extension, including a boundary correction and a 61-node distributive lattice. The full lattice is checked at three primes; a general characteristic-zero lift is still open. This uploaded snapshot ends at 07C.

External mathematical inputs include Thomas Lam, [Matroids and amplitudes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.06705), and Christopher Eur and Thomas Lam, [Canonical forms of oriented matroids](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.20782). Their constructions and standard amplitude identities must be credited separately from any new EHF grouping or deletion result.

## Relationship to newer probes

The source documents address cycle objects, growth, constrained dynamics, and amplitude relations. [QM revisits 01–03](../../probes/qm-bridge/README.md) test smaller specified MICRO–OPW23 carriers and retain exact classical controls. Recovering these sources neither invalidates those bounded tests nor extends their conclusions to OPW3 invariant sectors. A shared state description alone does not supply quantum evolution or measurement.

No new probe was run as part of this source import. A proposed general admissibility rule for candidate reversal lengths remains a research question, not an established unification. Before extending the programme, map assumptions and explicit maps, recover missing certificates, and distinguish reproduced results from source-reported ones.
