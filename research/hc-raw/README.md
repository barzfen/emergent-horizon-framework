## Verification limits

The available Python files for **RAW-12, RAW-13 and RAW-14 only print explanatory statements and a PASS message**. They do not execute the claimed certification. Their notes and CSV ledgers are useful historical records, but these files must not be cited as a reproduced exhaustive computation. Earlier RAW scripts have varying scopes and have not been rerun merely because they are now included.

The import [manifest and syntax/integrity checks](../consolidation-2026-11-02/README.md) distinguish source preservation from scientific verification. Reported results in the working documents retain their original status pending targeted checking. Admissible comparison and the Hamilton-cycle ansatz remain chosen mathematical starting data; they are not demonstrated fundamental substances of nature.
