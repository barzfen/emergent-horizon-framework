# EHF-XBR-04C — Exact Restricted-Action Certificate

## Purpose

EHF-XBR-04 determined the minimal OPW3 adjacency Krylov closure generated by each canonical deletion-adjoint AMP image. EHF-XBR-04B then certified every closure dimension and first stabilization depth over \(\mathbb Q\).

EHF-XBR-04C converts those rank statements into explicit invariant-subspace witnesses.

For each channel \(A\), let \(G_A\) be the primitive-integer lifted AMP image and let

\[
\mathcal K_A=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb Q}\{G_A,G_A\operatorname{Adj}_A,G_A\operatorname{Adj}_A^2,\ldots\}.
\]

The probe extracts an exact row basis \(V_A\) of \(\mathcal K_A\) and constructs exact rational matrices \(R_A\) and \(C_A\) satisfying

\[
V_A\operatorname{Adj}_A=R_A V_A,
\qquad
G_A=C_A V_A.
\]

This is the row-vector equivalent of the column-vector intertwiner identity

\[
\operatorname{Adj}_A V_A^{\mathsf T}
=V_A^{\mathsf T}R_A^{\mathsf T}.
\]

## Exact checks

For every nonzero channel, 04C verifies:

1. the source XBR-04B rational certificate passed;
2. the source fibre is exactly one OPW3 connected chamber;
3. local OPW adjacency is symmetric;
4. \(\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb Q}(V_A)=\dim\mathcal K_A\);
5. the basis dimension equals the XBR-04B-certified closure dimension;
6. the next Krylov block has the same exact rank;
7. the selected basis reaches the certified first stabilization depth;
8. a selected square pivot minor of \(V_A\) has nonzero determinant;
9. \(G_A=C_A V_A\) exactly over \(\mathbb Q\);
10. \(V_A\operatorname{Adj}_A=R_A V_A\) exactly over \(\mathbb Q\);
11. optionally, with \(H_A=V_A V_A^{\mathsf T}\),

\[
R_AH_A=H_AR_A^{\mathsf T},
\]

which certifies self-adjointness of the restricted action in the induced Gram form.

The four exact zero-image channels receive trivial zero-closure certificates.

## Deterministic basis convention

The block-Krylov rows are ordered by:

1. Krylov depth;
2. original lifted-image generator index.

The basis consists of the pivot columns of the transpose of the complete Krylov matrix through the certified stabilization depth. This chooses a deterministic subset of the original integer Krylov rows rather than replacing them by an arbitrary reduced basis.
