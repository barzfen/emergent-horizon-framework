# Focused literature comparison — 19 October 2026

This is a scope comparison for the working draft, not an exhaustive novelty determination. See the manuscript for complete bibliographic details.

| Source | Relationship to this draft | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Mallows and Shepp (2008), The necklace process | Their gap-uniform insertion is white exactly between two black neighbours. Our admissible gap-colour events forbid isolated beads of either colour and preserve run count. | The original process has a different rule. This does not establish priority for our model. |
| Nakata (2009), Necklace Processes Via Pólya Urns | Publisher abstract describes a classification of generalized necklace processes with urns and run statistics. Full text was not retrieved. | Closest unresolved comparison; do not claim novelty of the model or urn reduction. |
| Hackl and Prodinger (2018) | Generating-function treatment of the Mallows–Shepp process. | Related necklace literature, with a different starting rule. |
| Turowski, Jacquet and Szpankowski (2019), Theorem 2 | Dirichlet-multinomial entropy has leading term (d−1) log t. | The leading logarithmic scaling here is established background, not a new general law. |
| Boyd, Diaconis, Parrilo and Xiao; Niepert | Established symmetry and orbit methods for Markov chains. | Neither quotienting nor symmetry reduction itself is claimed new. |
| Geiger and Temmel | Lumping and entropy-rate preservation for finite-state chains. | Distinguish entropy rate from this draft's changing-layer endpoint entropy. |

Candidate contributions requiring literature and external proof review are the explicit single-seed quotient-entropy decrease, the minimal four-run all-time theorem, and their model-specific periodicity analysis. The finite many-domain scans are supporting computations.
