# EHF Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Consolidation

- Inventory the legacy EHF directory before deleting anything local.
- Classify every retained result by evidence status.
- Merge duplicate and superseded documents.
- Preserve negative results where they constrain future work.
- Keep generated data only when it is expensive or impossible to reproduce.

## Phase 2 — Formal core

- Define configurations, comparisons, admissibility, transformations, histories, growth, records, and observers precisely.
- Separate definitions from physical interpretations.
- Promote repeated computational observations to theorem candidates where possible.

## Phase 3 — Effective-law programme

Highest-priority test: derive recognizable macroscopic dynamics from microscopic rules.

Targets:

1. spectral-gap scaling and transport;
2. diffusion from local admissible dynamics;
3. internal clocks from record structure;
4. topology-dependent sectors and interactions;
5. controlled quantum-like evolution, only if it can be derived rather than assumed.

## Phase 4 — Comparison and falsification

Compare EHF constructions against established frameworks including causal sets, dynamical graph models, rewriting systems, quantum walks, statistical mechanics, and discrete quantum geometry.

Every proposed physical interpretation should include a null model and an explicit failure criterion.

- Next Wheeler probe: WHEELER-59, root/rotation quotient, measure covariance, lumpability and entropy.
- Separately repair the growth/comparison clock connection with an explicit combined generator.
- Replace infinite-history/one-event cost proxies with a finite-horizon inference experiment before claiming an optimal observer budget.

- Build a master index of FOUND, MICRO, RECUR, FILT, SD, COMP, SUB, and related probe families from the legacy archive.
